EXPERIMENT 3 - YOLOV8S AT IMGSZ=1280
========================================

Purpose:
    Test whether a larger model (yolov8s, ~11M params) adds
    performance on top of the resolution gain already established in
    Experiment 2.

Setup:
    Model:        YOLOv8s (11,139,470 params, 28.7 GFLOPs)
    Pretraining:  COCO
    Dataset:      VisDrone2019-DET (train 6471 / val 548)
    Epochs:       30
    Batch size:   8
    Input size:   1280
    Hardware:     Tesla T4
    Runtime:      3.263 hours (~6.3 min/epoch)
    Peak VRAM:    ~14.3 GB (no OOM)

Overall metrics (best.pt):
    mAP50-95     0.331     (Exp 2: 0.278, +19%)
    mAP50        0.543     (Exp 2: 0.468, +16%)
    Precision    0.625
    Recall       0.512

Per-class mAP50-95 / mAP50 (best.pt):
    car                0.633  /  0.879
    bus                0.494  /  0.666
    motor              0.312  /  0.635
    pedestrian         0.319  /  0.643
    van                0.411  /  0.567
    truck              0.346  /  0.502
    tricycle           0.269  /  0.454
    people             0.215  /  0.512
    awning-tricycle    0.143  /  0.239
    bicycle            0.167  /  0.331

Per-class gains vs Exp 2 (mAP50):
    bicycle            +50%
    tricycle           +30%
    awning-tricycle    +24%
    truck              +22%
    people             +19%
    pedestrian         +16%
    motor              +16%
    van                +12%
    bus                +8%
    car                +4%

Conclusion:
    Model capacity contributes on top of resolution, but smaller than
    resolution. Exp 1->2 (resolution): +68% mAP50. Exp 2->3 (capacity):
    +16% for ~3.7x parameters. Both compound: 0.279 -> 0.543.
    Capacity gains concentrated in small/thin classes.
