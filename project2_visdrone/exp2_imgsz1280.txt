EXPERIMENT 2 - YOLOV8N AT IMGSZ=1280
========================================

Purpose:
    Test whether doubling input resolution from 640 to 1280 improves
    detection of small objects on VisDrone.

Setup:
    Model:        YOLOv8n (same as Exp 1, pretrained COCO)
    Dataset:      VisDrone2019-DET (train 6471 / val 548)
    Epochs:       30
    Batch size:   8 (reduced to fit 1280px)
    Input size:   1280  (ONLY CHANGE vs Exp 1)
    Hardware:     Tesla T4
    Runtime:      2.954 hours (~5.5 min/epoch)

Overall metrics (best.pt):
    mAP50-95     0.278     (Exp 1: 0.157, +77%)
    mAP50        0.468     (Exp 1: 0.279, +68%)
    Precision    0.562
    Recall       0.451

Per-class mAP50-95 / mAP50 (best.pt):
    Class             Exp2 mAP50     Exp1 mAP50    Fold
    car                0.845           0.712        1.2x
    bus                0.619           0.364        1.7x
    motor              0.549           0.306        1.8x
    pedestrian         0.553           0.289        1.9x
    people             0.431           0.214        2.0x
    van                0.506           0.314        1.6x
    truck              0.411           0.261        1.6x
    tricycle           0.349           0.193        1.8x
    awning-tricycle    0.193           0.082        2.4x
    bicycle            0.221           0.054        4.1x

Observations:
    - LARGEST gains in small/thin classes: bicycle 4.1x, awning-
      tricycle 2.4x, people 2.0x, pedestrian 1.9x.
    - Smallest gain in car (1.2x) — already near saturation.
    - Training was still improving at epoch 30.

Conclusion:
    Input resolution was the single biggest lever for VisDrone
    detection. Doubling resolution nearly doubled mAP50 and produced
    2-4x improvement on small classes.
