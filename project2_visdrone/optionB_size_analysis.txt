OPTION B - SIZE-STRATIFIED ANALYSIS OF EXP 1 vs EXP 2
=========================================================

Purpose:
    Test whether the improvement from 640px (Exp 1) to 1280px (Exp 2)
    is concentrated in small objects.

Method:
    1. Compute median ground-truth box size per class on val set.
    2. Group 10 VisDrone classes into COCO size buckets:
         small  = median area < 32^2 px^2  (1024)
         medium = median area < 96^2 px^2  (9216)
         large  = median area >= 96^2 px^2
    3. Evaluate Exp 1 (640) and Exp 2 (1280) on val.
    4. Average mAP50-95 per bucket.
    5. Compare per bucket.

Median ground-truth box size per class:
    class              n     median px^2   median px side
    pedestrian      8844        295.0          17.2
    people          5125        280.0          16.7
    bicycle         1287        432.0          20.8
    car            14064       1056.0          32.5
    van             1975       1128.0          33.6
    truck            750       1491.0          38.6
    tricycle        1045        805.0          28.4
    awning-tricycle  532        858.0          29.3
    bus              251       1242.0          35.2
    motor           4886        400.0          20.0

Bucket assignments:
    small  (6 classes): pedestrian, people, bicycle, tricycle,
                        awning-tricycle, motor
    medium (4 classes): car, van, truck, bus
    large  (0 classes): (none - VisDrone has no COCO-large objects)

Bucket-level results:
    bucket    n   Exp1 mAP50-95   Exp2 mAP50-95   gain    fold
    small     6      0.079           0.181        +0.102  2.29x
    medium    4      0.273           0.414        +0.141  1.51x
    large     0      --              --           --      --

Per-class fold gains (mAP50-95):
    bicycle         5.26x
    people          2.35x
    pedestrian      2.27x
    awning-tricycle 2.25x
    motor           2.14x
    tricycle        1.88x
    bus             1.75x
    van             1.67x
    truck           1.62x
    car             1.28x

Conclusion:
    Small bucket improved 2.29x; medium bucket 1.51x. The larger
    improvement for smaller objects directly confirms the resolution
    hypothesis. VisDrone has NO "large" objects by COCO standards,
    explaining why resolution is such a powerful lever.
